# EverXCloud

[EverXCloud](https://everxcloud.ai/en) is a US GPU marketplace. Developers and teams rent NVIDIA GPUs by the hour
from reviewed suppliers, and GPU owners list their machines and earn from every hour sold.
EverXCloud Inc. is the merchant of record on every rental: one prepaid USD balance, one invoice, tax included in the price shown.

- **Rent GPUs:** [live marketplace](https://everxcloud.ai/en/market) with On-Demand, Time-Sliced, Pay per task, and Reserved [rental models](https://everxcloud.ai/en/formulas)
- **Sell GPU time:** [list your GPUs](https://everxcloud.ai/en/sell). Rentals that start before January 12, 2027 are commission-free for as long as they run
- **Build on it:** [REST API, scoped keys, and signed webhooks](https://everxcloud.ai/en/developers)
- **Plan a workload:** [LLM VRAM calculator](https://everxcloud.ai/en/tools/llm-vram-calculator) and [GPU specs](https://everxcloud.ai/en/gpu)
